🛡️ Anti-Bot Protection (GEM System)

🎯 Optional Feature for Token Creators

When enabled, this smart contract-based system protects your community from bot frontrunning while funding airdrops for genuine supporters. Creators can enable this during token creation as part of Advanced Options.

How It Works

The GEM System implements time-based KAS fee decay that makes bot frontrunning unprofitable while rewarding patient, genuine community members. All anti-bot fees are collected and distributed back to the community through our Airdrop Treasury.

📊 Fee Schedule (Linear Decay)

Formula: fee(t) = 95 - (94 × t / 60) where t = seconds since token launch

  • t = 0 seconds: 95% fee - Bots pay maximum penalty
  • t = 10 seconds: 80% fee - Still very high cost
  • t = 20 seconds: 65% fee - Rapidly decreasing
  • t = 30 seconds: 50% fee - Halfway point
  • t = 40 seconds: 35% fee - Approaching normal
  • t = 50 seconds: 20% fee - Nearly complete
  • t = 60+ seconds: 1% fee - Permanent normal trading (standard platform fee)

💡 The Math Works

The fee decay is perfectly linear over 60 seconds. Bots racing to be first pay the highest price, while genuine community members who wait just one minute trade at normal fees forever.

🎁 Anti-Bot Fee Distribution (On-Chain Transparent Split)

Anti-bot fees are split automatically at the smart contract level for complete transparency:

  • 70% → Airdrop Treasury: Leaderboard rewards for top traders, creators, and community contributors
  • 30% → Platform Development: Security audits, infrastructure costs, and platform maintenance

🔍 Why Split at Contract Level?

Transparency: The split happens on-chain in the immutable smart contract. No manual transfers, no intermediary wallets. Anyone can verify the exact 70/30 split by viewing blockchain transactions and contract events.

Clean Optics: Funds flow directly to their final destinations. You'll never see transfers from the airdrop treasury to dev wallets (which would be a red flag). Everything is transparent and trustless.

⚡ Why This Works on Kaspa

Kaspa's unique BlockDAG architecture with ~100ms block times makes time-based protection highly effective:

  • 100ms blocks: Kaspa produces blocks 10x per second, enabling precise time-based enforcement
  • Time-based vs Block-based: Unlike Ethereum where blocks vary (12-15s), Kaspa's consistent speed allows accurate time tracking
  • No mempool sniping: Fast finality means bots can't easily frontrun transactions
  • Low fees: Even with anti-bot protection, base transaction costs remain minimal

🚀 Smart Contract Implementation

The anti-bot system is implemented at the smart contract level, not in the application. Once enabled, it runs automatically and trustlessly for the full 60-second launch window.

📈 Charting & Position Tracking

🎯 Professional-Grade Analytics

Gemlaunch.fun features an elite position tracking system with FTX-style average-cost rebasing. This isn't just a chart - it's a complete trading analytics platform that shows your true profitability across all your trades.

💎 FTX-Style Cost Basis Rebasing

Our position tracking system uses the same weighted average methodology used by professional exchanges like FTX, Binance, and Coinbase. This ensures accurate P&L calculations across multiple buys and sells.

How It Works:

📊 Buying Tokens (Weighted Average)

Algorithm: cost_basis += KAS_spent; avg_entry = cost_basis / total_tokens

Example:

  • Buy 100 tokens @ 1 KAS each → cost_basis = 100 KAS, avg_entry = 1.00 KAS
  • Buy 100 tokens @ 3 KAS each → cost_basis = 400 KAS, avg_entry = 2.00 KAS

Your average entry price is the weighted average of all your purchases. This is your true "break-even" price.

💰 Selling Tokens (Rebasing)

Critical Insight: When you sell, we subtract the original cost of those tokens, NOT the sale proceeds.

Algorithm: cost_basis -= avg_entry × tokens_sold

Example:

  • Position: 200 tokens, cost_basis = 400 KAS, avg_entry = 2.00 KAS
  • Sell 50 @ 5 KAS each → cost_basis = 400 - (2 × 50) = 300 KAS
  • New position: 150 tokens, cost_basis = 300 KAS, avg_entry = 2.00 KAS ✅

Why this matters: Rebasing ensures your average entry stays accurate when you take profits. Without it, selling at a profit would artificially inflate your average cost!

🎁 Airdrop Handling (Free Tokens = Lower Avg Cost)

Smart Economics: Airdrops are added to your position with $0 cost basis, which lowers your average entry price.

Example:

  • Buy 100 @ 2 KAS = 200 KAS cost, avg = 2.00 KAS
  • Receive 100 airdrop (free)
  • New position: 200 tokens, 200 KAS cost, avg = 1.00 KAS 🎉

This shows the true value of community engagement - free tokens from airdrops reduce your average cost and increase your profit margin!

💹 P&L Calculations

Realized P&L (Locked In Profits/Losses):

Formula: (sell_price - avg_entry) × quantity_sold

  • Calculated and locked in every time you sell
  • Tracks your actual profits withdrawn from the position
  • Displayed in your position summary
  • Shows cumulative profits across all sells

Unrealized P&L (Paper Profits/Losses):

Formula: (current_price - avg_entry) × tokens_remaining

  • Shows profit/loss on your current holdings
  • Updates in real-time as price changes
  • Green = profit, Red = loss
  • Percentage gain/loss displayed alongside dollar amount

  • Future price increases calculate correctly from your true entry point
  • No artificial inflation of your cost basis (unlike naive systems)
