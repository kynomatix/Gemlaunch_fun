  • Team Allocations: Time-locked vesting for founders, developers, advisors

  • DAO Treasury: Build governance reserves for community-driven decisions
  • Marketing Campaigns: Strategic token distribution for organic growth

💡 Pro tip

Reserved tokens remain locked in smart contracts until activated - they can't be dumped or manipulated

🏗️ Complete Launch Process

1. Token Creation - Set name, symbol, image, and choose supply/reserves (takes 30 seconds)

2. Fair Launch - Token starts at algorithmic ~$1,000 market cap (no upfront capital required)

3. Price Discovery - Price increases automatically as people buy (xy=k mathematical curve)

4. Community Building - Share, promote, trade - earn GEM points for activity

5. Graduation - At ~$70k market cap, auto-lists on Kaspa Finance DEX (liquidity permanently locked)
