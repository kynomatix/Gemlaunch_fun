Gemlaunch Anti-Bot System - Final Summary
Core Mechanism: Time-Based KAS Fee Decay
60-Second Linear Decay Schedule:
t=0s:   95% fee (instant snipe = lose 95% of KAS)
t=10s:  80% fee
t=20s:  65% fee
t=30s:  50% fee (break-even zone)
t=40s:  30% fee
t=50s:  15% fee
t=60s:  1% fee (normal trading begins)
t=60s+: 1% fee (permanent normal rate)
How It Works

User wants to buy tokens

Sends 100 KAS to bonding curve at t=5s


Anti-bot fee calculated

t=5s fee ≈ 88% (linear interpolation)
88 KAS → Airdrop Treasury
12 KAS → Bonding curve (buys tokens)


User receives tokens

Gets 12 KAS worth of tokens
Paid 100 KAS total
Not profitable for bots



Why This Works on Kaspa (100ms blocks)

⚡ Time-based, not block-based - 60 seconds = 600 blocks
🎯 Eliminates speed advantage - bots can't profit by being fast
🎲 Game theory optimal - everyone waits, leveling the playing field
💰 Economic deterrent - real KAS cost, not just token loss
⏱️ Short enough - maintains launch excitement
⏱️ Long enough - humans can compete
