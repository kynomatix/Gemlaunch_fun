GemLaunch.fun Documentation
🎯 Project Overview
Vision: Bring the proven pump.fun model to the underserved Kaspa ecosystem with community-first economics and professional growth tools.

Target: Kaspa community hungry for memecoin infrastructure (first-mover advantage)

Key Differentiator: Complete ecosystem from fair meme launches to professional project tools - community-owned, transparent fee sharing, ecosystem reinvestment vs extraction

Partnership: Integrated with Kaspa Finance - all graduated tokens automatically deploy to their DEX with professional liquidity pools

📊 Platform Mechanics & Quick Stats
Bonding Curve Model
Starting Market Cap: ~$1,000 (algorithmic, not user funds - creators only pay gas fees)
Graduation Threshold: ~$70,000 market cap (automatic DEX listing with locked liquidity)
Token Supply: 1B tokens (default) - fully customizable if desired
Platform Fee: 1% (community-aligned distribution, not extraction)
Bonding Curve: x*y=k formula (proven mathematical model for fair pricing)
First-Mover Advantage: Only memecoin launchpad on Kaspa ecosystem
Token Creation Options
Our hybrid approach gives creators maximum flexibility:

🎯 Simple Mode (Default)

1 billion token supply - industry standard, no thinking required
No technical decisions needed - just name, image, and launch
One-click deployment for quick meme launches
⚙️ Advanced Mode (Optional)

Custom token supply - set any amount from 1M to 100B+ tokens
Token reserves - hold back up to 25% for future utility (locked until you use professional tools)
Strategic planning for projects that might evolve beyond memes
Reserved Token Use Cases
When creators choose to reserve tokens (up to 25% of supply), they can later deploy them through our gemlaunch.io professional suite:

Community Airdrops: Reward loyal holders and attract new users
Team Allocations: Time-locked vesting for founders, developers, advisors
Partnership Incentives: Collaborate with other projects and platforms
DAO Treasury: Build governance reserves for community-driven decisions
Marketing Campaigns: Strategic token distribution for organic growth
💡 Pro tip: Reserved tokens remain locked in smart contracts until activated - they can't be dumped or manipulated

How The Complete Process Works
Token Creation: Set name, symbol, image, and choose supply/reserves (takes 30 seconds)
Fair Launch: Token starts at algorithmic ~$1,000 market cap (no upfront capital required)
Price Discovery: Price increases automatically as people buy (xy=k mathematical curve)*
Community Building: Share, promote, trade - earn GEM points for activity
Graduation: At ~$70k market cap, auto-lists on Kaspa Finance DEX (liquidity permanently locked)
Evolution: Optionally access gemlaunch.io professional suite for reserved tokens and advanced features
Fee Structure (1% swap fee)
Based on LetsBonk.fun's successful model:

40% Platform Development: Features, security, mobile support
30% GEM Buyback & Burn: Deflationary pressure on GEM token
20% Kaspa Network Support: Validator support, infrastructure
10% Community Rewards: GEM airdrops for contributors
Creator Revenue Share
Unlike Pump.fun, creators receive a portion of KAS contributed to their bonding curve (similar to LetsBonk.fun model).
